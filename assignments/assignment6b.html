I liked using the HTML Validator because it helped me find and fix my mistakes. Sometimes I did not notice small errors, such as extra spaces or missing opening and closing HTML tags. These were minor mistakes, but the validator made them easy to find and correct. I also learned the importance of writing good ALT text for images. Good ALT text helps people who use screen readers understand the content of images and makes websites more accessible for everyone. This assignment taught me that validating HTML and checking accessibility are important steps in creating a professional, user-friendly, and error-free website.
