I completed the LearnFree Internet Safety tutorial and learned many important ways to stay safe while using the Internet. The tutorial explained common online threats such as phishing, malware, viruses, spyware, and ransomware. I learned that phishing emails and fake websites are designed to trick users into revealing personal information, such as passwords or banking details. I also learned that malware can infect a computer through unsafe downloads, suspicious email attachments, or untrusted websites. Another important lesson was creating strong and unique passwords for every online account and changing them regularly. The tutorial also explained why it is important to avoid sharing personal information online and to recognize warning signs before clicking unknown links. Overall, this activity increased my awareness of online security risks and taught me practical steps to protect my personal information, devices, and online accounts.

I reviewed the privacy and security settings in Google Chrome and practiced several browser safety activities. First, I checked that my browser was updated to the latest version because updates include important security patches that protect against newly discovered vulnerabilities. Next, I reviewed my browser extensions and disabled any add-ons that I did not need to reduce security risks. I cleared my browsing history, cache, and cookies to remove stored information and improve privacy. I also reviewed the autofill settings and removed personal information that I did not want the browser to save. After that, I visited a secure website using HTTPS and viewed its security certificate to understand how SSL/TLS encrypts information between my browser and the website. I also used Incognito mode to visit a website that normally remembers me and noticed that I was not automatically signed in. Finally, I learned about antivirus software, firewalls, password managers, and multi-factor authentication (MFA). These tools provide additional protection by detecting malware, blocking unauthorized access, securely storing passwords, and requiring an extra verification step during login. Overall, these activities helped me better understand browser security, privacy settings, and safe Internet practices.
